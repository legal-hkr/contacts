v0.1.0

First version:
- Contact list with favorites, letters and colored avatars, and search in every field
- Contact page and editor with photos, default numbers and every kind of detail
- Accounts: show or hide each one, local contacts on the phone, moving to an account
- Duplicates merged permanently or only joined
- vCard import (2.1, 3.0 and 4.0) and export of all contacts
- No Internet access: syncing is left to the apps that manage your accounts

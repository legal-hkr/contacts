Contacts is a private address book for Android. It shows and changes the contacts kept in the phone’s own contacts storage and has no access to the Internet: syncing is left to the apps that manage your accounts, for example DAVx⁵ with Nextcloud or any other CardDAV server. No account of its own, no ads, no analytics.

List and search:
- Favorites at the top, then the contacts under their letters, sorted by last name or by first name
- Photos, or colored avatars with initials
- A fast scroller that shows the current letter
- Search in names, nicknames, numbers, email addresses, companies and notes, with the match highlighted; numbers are found with or without the country code

Contact page and editor:
- Call, message and email in one tap, with one default number for calls and messages
- Numbers, email addresses, addresses, websites, dates, relationships, messaging and SIP addresses, notes; touch and hold to copy
- Every kind of detail in the editor, with your own labels, and a photo picked and cropped on the phone
- “Create contact” from other apps, such as the phone app
- Deleting with Undo; sharing as a vCard file

Accounts:
- Show or hide each account’s contacts; messaging apps’ accounts start hidden
- Contacts kept only on the phone, which can be moved to an account, with Undo
- When an account’s server doesn’t accept a change or a deletion, as with a read-only address book, the app says so

Duplicates:
- Suggestions by a shared number or email address, never by a name alone
- Permanent merging into one contact, choosing what stays, or only joining the entries
- “Not the same person” answers are kept by the phone’s contacts storage, not by the app

Import and export:
- vCard files 2.1, 3.0 and 4.0, opened from another app, shared, or picked, including the character sets of older phones
- Contacts already on the phone are marked; an import can be undone
- Export of all contacts, or of chosen accounts, to one vCard file, with or without photos

And also:
- Backup and restore of settings to a file you choose; contacts are never in it
- English and Polish, independent of the system language
- Material 3 Expressive design with your phone’s colors
